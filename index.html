Common questions

Do you support businesses outside Dublin?

Yes. Most issues can be fixed remotely anywhere in Ireland, and we travel on site when hands-on work is needed.

We're a small team. Is this for us?

Yes — we work with small and growing businesses that don't have their own IT department and want one reliable contact.

What happens in the free IT review?

We look at your devices, accounts, network, backups and security settings, then give you a short, plain-English report of risks and next steps.

Can you support our POS and card terminals?

Yes. We install and support POS systems, the networks they run on, and the payment security around them.
